PolySec Cybersecurity Lab, Cal Poly Pomona

Skills: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SFTP, CMS, Figma


Overview

I was tasked with updating the cybersecurity lab's website to showcase projects from the past five years. This involved not only organizing and displaying research content but also enhancing the visual appeal of the site with photography, custom logos, and new design elements.

Research

To determine what content should be highlighted, I met with each project team to discuss their research goals and the aspects they wanted featured on the site. I reviewed existing site structure and content to identify gaps and redundancies, then explored ways to make the information more accessible and engaging for both technical and non-technical audiences.

Goals

  • Modernize the website's layout and navigation to improve user experience.
  • Accurately represent research work from the past five years.
  • Incorporate more visual storytelling to engage visitors.

Obstacles

  • Limited availability of lab supervisors for review and approvals during busy academic periods.
  • Need to balance creative updates with maintaining the lab's branding guidelines.

Solution

  • Used Figma to propose design changes, expediting approvals during tight supervisor schedules.
  • Consolidated and standardized project information for clarity and consistency.
  • Implemented JavaScript features to add interactive elements to the site.

Results

  • Delivered an updated, visually cohesive website that clearly communicates the lab's research activities.
  • Improved navigation and presentation for a better visitor experience.

Reflection

I'm thankful to the team for giving me creative freedom over design changes and trusting the vision 😎. My past Figma experience from hackathons proved invaluable when proposing ideas, especially when quick approvals were needed during busy weeks. Being able to experiment with JavaScript functionality and visual design made this project both a creative and technical learning experience.
